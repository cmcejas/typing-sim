# Typing-Sim
Typing simulator that makes typing look realistic and successfully dodges many bot trackers.
- includes typos and corrections
- varied typing speed at your own selection
- pauses at randomized intervals accurate to humans (simulating thinking)

Can be loaded onto usb and made to appear as a keyboard and autorun
